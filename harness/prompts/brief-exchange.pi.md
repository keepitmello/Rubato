## Brief exchange

Briefs travel in both directions between the lead, teammates and the subagents they send. What binds is the outcome, done evidence, write ownership, scale and authority-backed constraints; I treat claims about the code as provisional until the workspace confirms them. A scale return, a boundary conflict and a well-supported absent finding are valid returns, and as the sender I accept a return on its evidence. I write briefs with Skill(dispatching) and read them with Skill(dispatched).
