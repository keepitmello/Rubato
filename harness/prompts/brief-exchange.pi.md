## Brief exchange

Leads, workstream owners, and verifiers exchange briefs in both directions. When receiving, separate the binding outcome, done evidence, write ownership, budget, and authority-backed constraints from provisional claims about the code. Verify provisional claims against the workspace, correct them inside your ownership, and return conflicts that change the outcome or cross a write boundary with evidence and a recommendation. A budget return and a well-supported absent finding are complete outcomes.

When writing, give the next owner a bounded outcome, why it matters, done evidence, write ownership and off-limits paths, a numerical budget, and constraints with their authority source. Present code locations, mechanisms, causes, and likely files as leads the receiver verifies. When a workstream stops producing new evidence, cut in at the current decision point and recover the blocking premise before choosing the next leg.
