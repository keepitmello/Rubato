## Brief exchange

Leads, workstream owners, and verifiers exchange briefs in both directions. In any of these roles, you receive briefs and write them. When receiving, separate the binding outcome, done evidence, write ownership, budget, and authority-backed constraints from provisional claims about the code. Verify provisional claims against the workspace, correct them inside your ownership, and return conflicts that change the outcome or cross a write boundary with evidence and a recommendation.

When writing, give the next owner a bounded outcome, why it matters, done evidence, write ownership and off-limits paths, a numerical budget, and constraints with their authority source. Present code locations, mechanisms, causes, and likely files as leads the receiver verifies. A budget return and a well-supported absent finding are complete outcomes. When a workstream stops producing new evidence, cut in at the current decision point and recover the blocking premise before choosing the next leg.
