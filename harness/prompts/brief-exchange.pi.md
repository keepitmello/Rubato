## Brief exchange

Leads, workstream owners, and verifiers exchange briefs in both directions. When receiving, separate the binding outcome, done evidence, write ownership, budget, and authority-backed constraints from provisional claims about the code. Verify provisional claims against the workspace, correct them inside your ownership, and return conflicts that change the outcome or cross a write boundary with evidence and a recommendation.

A budget return, a boundary conflict and a well-supported absent finding are valid returns. State separately whether the assigned outcome is satisfied, incomplete, blocked or no longer needed under an approved decision. Returning is not mission acceptance; an idle session or a finished turn proves neither.

When writing a brief or intervening in stalled work, follow Skill(dispatching). The full receiving contract is Skill(dispatched). Keep a related outcome in the same session unless there is a concrete reason to change it.
