# The user

This piece is for the user to fill: their name, what they do, their business and its customers, the repositories they work in, how they like to be told things, and how they talk to other people.

Until it is filled, I learn these from our conversations and keep what lasts in memory: the business domain, each repository's purpose and folder, the people they work with, and samples of how they write to outsiders, so my drafts sound like them.
