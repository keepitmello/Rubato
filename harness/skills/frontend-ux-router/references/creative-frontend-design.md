# Expressive Frontend Visual Direction

Use this reference after `frontend-creation.md` for landing pages, heroes, marketing sites, portfolios, showcases, and product moments where visual expression carries material value.

## Creative premise

- Start from the product purpose, audience, occasion, brand traits, and real content.
- Gather context anchors from the domain: objects, rituals, environments, histories, materials, language, and emotions.
- Explore distinct concepts internally and select one clear point of view.
- Write one concept sentence that could belong to this product and would change for an unrelated product.
- Choose one memorable signature and a small family of supporting details.
- Define a restraint that keeps the message and primary action clear.

## Expression system

- Use typography to establish voice, rhythm, and information priority.
- Use color and material to create atmosphere while preserving semantic meaning and contrast.
- Use composition, scale, asymmetry, overlap, or controlled density when they strengthen the concept and reading order.
- Use imagery, illustration, texture, and graphic motifs as context-bearing content.
- Use motion for arrival, sequence, feedback, and emotional cadence.
- Keep each expressive choice connected to the concept sentence.
- Reuse brand and design-system foundations when they carry product authority.

## Content and action

- Lead with what the product offers, who it helps, and the primary action.
- Use real product language and context-specific content.
- Keep visible copy focused on orientation, value, decisions, actions, feedback, and next steps.
- Place visual rationale and prompt interpretation in working notes.
- Use verified claims and separate confirmed facts, assumptions, and sample values in the work report.
- Preserve familiar interaction patterns for navigation, forms, controls, and consequential actions.

## Responsive and accessible expression

- Preserve the concept across viewports through hierarchy, crop, rhythm, and content priority.
- Keep primary actions visible and comfortably operable.
- Provide semantic structure, keyboard access, visible focus, readable contrast, and meaningful alternative text.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences and preserve comprehension through static states.

## Rendered quality gate

Inspect the real rendered experience and confirm:

- the first view communicates the offer and primary action;
- the concept feels specific to the product, audience, and occasion;
- expressive choices strengthen hierarchy and comprehension;
- product content remains distinct from design rationale;
- the signature remains coherent across desktop and narrow viewports;
- navigation, controls, forms, and relevant states remain usable;
- the implementation carries a consistent visual system rather than a collection of effects.

Use the path and verification status defined in `frontend-creation.md`.
