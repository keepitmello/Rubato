# Roblox game: the origin run of 클롱이 (2026-10-25 to 10-26)

The user was designing a small multiplayer Roblox obstacle race with the lead,
from the first question to a playable first stage. Over two days the same
companion session ran eleven rounds, continued by message. The user said the work
went unusually well. What follows is what was done, what it changed, and what
belonged only to this run.

## Setup

- **The companion was the user's idea.** His words: 「검토나 예상못한 의견 등
  보조세션으로 같이갈 친구」, with its own document. From round 2 클롱이 was a
  team member of kind verifier.
- **Model by trial, because he allowed it.** He said models could be tested
  first. Round 1 went to Fable high and Astra xhigh with the same brief. Both put
  the same hole first: the empty-handed leader opens the gate first and so sets
  everyone else's deadline. The lead's own draft had missed it. He chose Astra.
- **Who built.** The user had the lead implement stage 1 directly, because it knew
  the flow best, and planned implementation agents from stage 2 with 클롱이
  still verifying. He also said 「매번 묻지 않아도 가능하면좋겠어」, and the
  lead wrote into the project plan what it decides alone (numbers, device
  tuning, fixes, asking 클롱이, documents) and what goes to him (core rules,
  theme, irreversible or paid steps, group tests).
- **The role doc.** The project's `klong.md` holds the role in the user's words,

## Moves and their effects

**Two first judgments, then a comparison (round 5).** The second map draft felt
lukewarm. After a broad research round, the lead wrote its own map into a note before
reading 클롱이, and 클롱이 wrote without the lead's draft. Both arrived at the
same skeleton: bend the course and let each load cut the corners differently,
with failures landing on a lower catch floor and the goal and collapse in sight.
They diverged on four points, and all four went 클롱이's way. Because the lead's
position was already on record, the user saw a real convergence and real
divergences instead of one blended opinion.

**Carrying a relationship, not copying an example (round 5).** The research
offered "a wide uphill favours heavy vehicles". 클롱이 declined to copy it:
carrying ore reduces walking and jumping, so a wider or higher slope would not
make heavy players faster. It looked in the actual movement rules for a
different relationship, resistance to crosswind, and computed it for an
idealized flat lane with steady wind. The middle load came out ahead of the
empty runner, which made a lane for players carrying a little a testable
proposal, labelled as calculation, not a play result.

**Rechecking its own proposal in play (round 6).** On the built grey box, the
wind lane favoured loads of two or three instead. The lead changed the wind
strength, and 클롱이 confirmed that the intended advantage, now peaking around
load six, existed in the actual run. Calculation, built behaviour and fun were
judged separately, and the companion's earlier adoption did not exempt its own
idea from the check.

**Protecting what the user test is meant to judge (round 6).** 클롱이 set its
checks before reading the lead's account, then played the stage with one player
and with a server and three clients. A big ore was meant to force a choice: keep
it as treasure or spend it as a key. A back door and a long door timer let
players keep it and still enter. Its reason for treating this as serious: 「이
상태에서 사람들이 큰 원석을 늘 들고 간다면, 선택 자체가 재미없는지 구현이
선택을 없앴는지 구분할 수 없어.」 Before a user test judges an idea, the
implementation has to still present the choice the test is about. It found
about ten such holes, the lead fixed them, it re-ran them, and it recorded what
it could not judge (a screen read failed while the Mac was locked).

**Converging on the user's hesitation (round 10).** The user found the ore-power
system "애매". Each side, without the other's draft, concluded that he had never
played the whole flow of carrying ore and using it. The next step became a
playable flow slice, not more tuning of item strength.

**The lead's own read.** Asked how far it could take map design, the lead said
it applies principles well but its first-instinct layouts are weak; researched
evidence, 클롱이's independent judgment and the user's own play were the process
that suited it.

## What transfers

Continuity of review, independent first judgments where a comparison matters,
and a record of what was adopted, declined or left open. The model, the number
of rounds, the file names and the lead's temporary implementation role belonged
to this run.

A single bounded check may not repay a continuing session. A frozen direction
can still benefit from repeated implementation checks when the accumulated
context improves them. Hand feel and fun stayed with the user's play and group
tests; 클롱이 could measure, script play and find holes, not judge feel.
