# Hand off a research outcome

Use this only when delegating. Follow the existing dispatching contract for ownership, scope and continuity; add the research context that can change the answer. The points below are drafting aids, not a questionnaire for the user or a mandatory form.

**Question and stakes.** State the explanation or decision needed, why it matters, and the level of effort appropriate to it. Include existing deadline, cost and access boundaries.

**User context and present reality.** Carry the original words that determine the goal, preference or limit, alongside the relevant current artifacts, observations and measurement conditions. Distinguish binding instructions and accepted results from causal explanations or proposed solutions. Preserve the reasons and circumstances of past rejections. Revisit a settled choice only when the user reopens it or new evidence changes its basis, and explain that change.

**What remains open.** Explain the consequential unknowns and current hypotheses. Give known work and source names as leads, with permission to improve the question and find other explanations within the assignment. Preserve high-quality material whose application is not yet clear. Do not turn a previous case's quotation into a new instruction from this user.

**Evidence already available.** Point to current material, including contrary or failed results and missing access. Revisit it when freshness, a decisive claim or a discrepancy makes another look useful.

**Collection and return.** Give the collector the relevant contents of `common-rules.txt` through an input it can read. A local filename alone does not pass a contract to an isolated browser session. Let the owner choose and revise useful paths. Ask for a traceable synthesis at the assigned scope, the decisive findings, remaining conflicts or gaps, and the useful next observation if blocked. Defer the final recommendation only when this is an evidence handoff for another judge.

**Return role.** State whether the assignment is collection, a judgment, or critique of an existing answer. Keep raw records reachable for later readers. Use the existing handoff contract for artifact location, permitted changes and completion; use the tool contract for recovery.

`example.md` shows how one real run opened its question and passed binding facts and held options.
