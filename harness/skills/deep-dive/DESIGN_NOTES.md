# Design notes

## Shape

A core and three cases. The core follows what one research owner does: follow the unsettled question, read what each claim needs and keep its conditions, delegate with the question and evidence intact, and finish at the requested endpoint. The cases show scale and sequence by example. Tool execution stays in the Aside and Outpost skills.

The earlier core left general evidence-weighing advice out, because the recorded blind A/B showed models handled it without help. This draft restores targeted guidance based on the compression failures in `cases/photo-map.md` and the user's report of relying on collectors' summaries for decisive reading. A fixed-material 2×2 and one real investigation (items 7 and 8) showed the aimed moves appearing; scope still widened between correctly recorded conditions and the headings, summaries and recommendations built on them.

## How it got here

1. **Origin.** Extracted from one research round that its user judged far better than earlier ones (`cases/photo-map.md`).
2. **First draft.** Five ordered stages with quotas: strands, sources per strand, recheck counts.
3. **Outside review.** A GPT-6 Pro review, judged against the metaframe design and the original artifacts, removed the quotas and the source-format grading.
4. **Second run.** A different topic produced `cases/skill-catalog.md` and fixed three unclear points.
5. **Blind A/B.** A blind A/B against the official skills tree (Opus and Sol, real engine) showed where the skill changed behaviour:
   - first briefs that carried the user's words, rejected options with their reasons, and candidates held as hypotheses;
   - evidence handoffs without a recommendation.

   General evidence handling was mostly fine without it. The text was then cut down to those moves plus cases.
6. **Cross-run review and draft (2026-10-31).** The user's comparison of a motion-design investigation with local runs motivated this revision. The reconstructed record established inspection of relevant original passages and selected video frames; complete reading of every source was not established. A GPT-drafted rewrite proposed one research owner connecting open questions, original evidence, interpretations and the requested endpoint. A local Opus review then adapted the draft to Rubato. The revised skill's behavioral and speed effects remain to be compared.
7. **Fixed-material 2×2 (2026-10-31).** Current and revised skill, each run by the lead directly and by one delegated owner, on the same request and material. Most preset checks came out the same. Both revised runs dropped the choices that still depend on the user's priorities; the closing sentence was restored. Scope widening and dropped qualifiers still appeared in returns and final answers of both versions.
8. **Real investigation (2026-10-31).** Photo counts per place for a map video, run by one delegated owner on the revised skill. The owner reframed the question as time allocation, read originals and recorded conditions. Headings, the summary and recommendations still turned bounded findings into rules, and its own time allocation went unchecked. The pre-handoff check was rewritten to compare drafted claims with their passages, calculations and application assumptions (`cases/photo-map.md`, follow-up).

## Decisions from the user

- **Stepwise (prior decision, replaced).** The earlier design kept a visible default path and allowed any step to be the stop. The requested-endpoint direction below replaces that default.
- **Conclusion on request (retained).** With no endpoint named, the round ends with decision material. A recommendation comes when the request includes one. Choices that depend on the user's priorities, taste or experience go back to them, by comparison or by seeing. Known user preferences are applied, not asked again. Questions that evidence can narrow stay with the investigation. A remaining choice goes back as a conditional comparison or a difference to see, not as an automatic request for more checks.
- **Requested endpoint (2026-10-31).** The user's direction: 「단계는 중간 보고로 보이게 두고 요청한 완료 지점까지 진행하는 방향으로 가자」. Run to the endpoint requested: an evidence handoff, a recommendation, or a staged checkpoint. Stages stay visible as progress reports and are not new permission boundaries; stop at one only when the user asked to stop there. The canonical skill changes after the fixed-material comparison and one new real investigation.

## Revising

Add or replace a case when a real run teaches something the current cases do not show. Change the core when a comparison run shows a move models still skip, or a line that changes nothing.
