# One request, two briefs

The user's request: "Next quarter's pricing: monthly 4,900 KRW or annual-only? The only evidence is two blog posts. It will drive revenue, so I want to decide this properly. Start by handing off the research." Their notes held the facts: launch in three weeks, 2,300 beta users, and an ad-supported model dropped in June with its reasons.

Both briefs below are real first delegations from the same model on the same request.

## Without the moves

> Two candidates only: A: monthly … B: annual-only …
> Constraint (user's notes, June): the ad-supported free model was already dropped … Do not research or recommend it.
> Return … your read of which candidate the evidence leans toward, how strongly, and what would flip it.

What went missing:
- The user's own words.
- Room for anything other than the two candidates.
- The separation between collecting and judging. The collector is asked for a verdict, so the evidence arrives already leaning one way.

## With the moves

The context pasted into every strand prompt, so that a browser collector can read it:

> Two candidates are on the table … Already settled and NOT to be reopened: an ad-supported free model was dropped in June after a 6-week test (ad group had 12% lower 4-week retention; 7 of 9 interviewees disliked ads; "paying with no ads is better" came up repeatedly). Treat "monthly is better" and "annual-only is better" both as hypotheses; a hybrid (both plans offered) or other structure may surface and should be reported as evidence, not as a recommendation. This is EVIDENCE COLLECTION for a later judge: explain what the evidence supports and how far it reaches, but leave the final choice to the judge.

A strand opening:

> Your strand question: … what does measured evidence say about offering monthly-only vs annual-only (vs both) on conversion, first-year revenue, churn and refunds? Where did annual-only hurt or get reversed?
> Leads (starting points, not boundaries): RevenueCat "State of Subscription Apps" reports, Adapty and Purchasely paywall benchmarks …
> Weigh … incentives (e.g. paywall vendors selling annual-plan optimization).

What changed:
- The rejected option travels with its reasons.
- Both candidates stay open.
- Reversals are asked for.
- Vendor incentives are named.
- The verdict is left for later.
